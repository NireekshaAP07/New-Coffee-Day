CHOOSE YOUR GRIND ☕

Same blend. Two ways to grind.

Choose the grind that suits your perfect cup. Our blends are available in Filter and Nice grinds, so you can pick the texture that feels right for your brewing style.

Selected Classic

Filter Grind

A slightly coarser grind made for traditional South Indian filter coffee. The larger particles allow a steady flow through the filter and give you a familiar, classic decoction.

Powder Texture Slightly coarser particles • Steady flow
Selected Fine & Smooth

Nice Grind

A finer, smoother grind with smaller particles for a richer, more intense decoction. Ideal if you prefer your coffee powder to have a softer, finer texture.

Powder Texture Fine & smooth particles • Rich decoction

Filter → slightly coarser • Nice → finer & smoother

FACTS ☕

Coffee facts, simply told

A few lovely things to know while your coffee is being made.

🫘

Coffee beans

Coffee “beans” are actually seeds found inside bright red coffee cherries.

🌱

Arabica & Robusta

Arabica is usually smoother; Robusta is known for its stronger, bolder character.

🔥

Roasting

Roasting brings out coffee’s colour, aroma, and the flavours we love in every cup.

🌾

Chicory

Roasted chicory adds body, gentle bitterness, and a familiar traditional depth.

✨

Coffee aroma

Much of coffee’s flavour is experienced through its aroma — that first lovely breath matters.

🌍

Coffee origins

Coffee is grown in warm regions around the world, each place bringing its own character.
